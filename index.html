Héctor Paredes

PhD candidate in Economics, Paris School of Economics & Université Paris 1 Panthéon-Sorbonne.
On the 2026-27 academic job market.

I work at the intersection of political economy, development and economic history. My dissertation, Essays on the Economic Drivers of Political Inequality (advisor: Oliver Vanden Eynde), studies how changes in the economic structure reshape local politics, with a focus on the 1969-80 Peruvian land reform. I also collaborate with academics, policymakers and international organizations on development issues, with a focus on agricultural economics. Before PSE, I was a research fellow at IPEG (UPF, Barcelona) and an assistant researcher at GRADE (Lima). MSc in Economics, Barcelona School of Economics.
